# 哪吒闹海：归海悟道

三年后，哪吒重返东海。海面波光粼粼，却不见昔日的喧嚣。他来到龙宫前，敖丙早已等候多时。

"you finally returned,"敖丙神色复杂，"during these three years， i've contemplated your words deeply。"

哪吒微笑："then what did you realize？"

"i believed dragons were superior，but discovered all beings are equal。i thought you challenged our dignity，when in fact you opposed injustice itself。"敖丙感慨万千。

"so what did you accomplish these three years？"

"i freed the imprisoned sea creatures，abolished unreasonable rules，and attempted to establish new order。"敖丙眼中闪烁着新的光芒，"but still feel something lacking。"

哪吒点头："what's missing is dao。true dao isn't overthrowing old order to build new，but allowing everyone to find their place。"

"have you found it now？"敖丙 asked。

哪吒望向远方："i'm still searching。rebellion isn't the end，but the beginning。true growth lies in finding balance between struggle and harmony。"

sea breeze gently brushed as they stood side by side。former enemies，now companions。which understood that true rebellion transforms rather than destroys；true growth fuses rather than conquers。

"let's go，" said to ao bing，"there are many unfinished paths ahead。"

waves softly拍，witnessing a new journey's start。
